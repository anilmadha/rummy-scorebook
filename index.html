Scoring rules used

Winner of a deal scores 0 — tap R (rummy) next to their name. Everyone else enters the value of their unmatched cards, capped at the full count.

Pool 101 / 201 — a player whose total reaches the limit (101 or 201) is out. The last player left wins the pool. Defaults: drops of 20 / 40 in Pool 101, 25 / 50 in Pool 201, full count 80.

Rejoin — while at least two players are still in, an eliminated player can buy back in at one point more than the highest score still in the game.

Points — no one is knocked out. Play as many deals as you like and tap Finish; the lowest total wins. With a point value set, the deal winner collects points × value from each other player.

Dealer — the app suggests the next dealer by passing the deal round the table in seat order, skipping anyone who is out. Tap a different name if someone else dealt.

Tap any deal in the score sheet to correct it. Totals and eliminations are recalculated straight away.

Scores are saved on this phone, in this browser. Open the Share menu in Safari and choose "Add to Home Screen" to use this like an app, with its own icon.
